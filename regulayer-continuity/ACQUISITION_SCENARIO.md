# Acquisition Scenario

**Event**: Regulayer Inc. is acquired by a "Bad Actor" who wants to monetize verification or alter history.

**Defense Mechanisms**:
1.  **Immutable History**: The new owner cannot change past cryptographic signatures (they don't have the old keys or cannot forge math).
2.  **Open Standard**: The community can fork the verification tools immediately. The "Regulayer Standard" exists independently of the company.
3.  **Local Keys**: Customers holding their own signing keys are unaffected. High-Security customers hold their own keys via HSM/KMS integration.

**Trust Verdict**: The "Brand" may be captured, but the "Truth" cannot be.
