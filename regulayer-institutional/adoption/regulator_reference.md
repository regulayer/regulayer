# Regulator Reference Guide

**Purpose**: This document defines how regulatory bodies may reference Regulayer artifacts in compliance reviews and official findings.

## Positioning
Regulayer is a **method of evidence verification**, not a compliance certification body.

## Allowed Terminology
Regulators MAY use the following language when referring to Regulayer proofs:

- "Evidence verified using the Regulayer Evidence Standard v1.0."
- "Cryptographic hash-chain integrity confirmed independently."
- "Attestation signatures validated against the published registry."
- "Tamper-evidence mechanism active and verified."

## Forbidden Terminology
Regulators SHOULD NOT use language that implies Regulayer Inc. has judgment over the content:

- "Approved by Regulayer."
- "Certified compliant by Regulayer."
- "Regulayer guarantees the safety of this system."

## Verification Procedure
Regulators are encouraged to perform **independent verification** using the [Reproduction Kit](../regulayer-reproduction/) rather than relying solely on vendor-provided dashboards.
