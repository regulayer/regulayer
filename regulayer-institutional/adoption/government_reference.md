# Government Reference Guide

**Purpose**: Guidance for government procurement and internal review usage.

## Infrastructure Classification
Regulayer should be classified as **Digital Trust Infrastructure** or **Audit Trail Middleware**. It is NOT a "Decision Support System" or "AI Model".

## Procurement & Sovereignty
- **Vendor Neutrality**: The standard allows for replacing the Regulayer vendor while keeping the verification methodology (Fork Policy).
- **Data Residency**: Verification can occur entirely offline or within sovereign clouds (`offline_years_later` scenario).

## Pilot Programs
Government pilots should focus on **high-stakes accountability** use cases (e.g., benefits determination, automated adjudication) where the *integrity of the record* is paramount.
