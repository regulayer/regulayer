# Long-Term Stewardship

**Mission**: To Ensure Verification Outlives the Vendor.

## The Time Horizon
Regulayer is designed for a **50-year horizon**. Evidence verified today must be verifiable by a court in 2075.

## Custodial Responsibility
Regulayer Inc. acts as the **Custodian** of the standard, not the owner of the truth. Truth belongs to the holder of the proof bundle.

## Key Continuity
We commit to maintaining the public availability of Root CA public headers and revocation lists for a minimum of 50 years, using distributed storage (IPFS/Arweave) if corporate infrastructure fails.
