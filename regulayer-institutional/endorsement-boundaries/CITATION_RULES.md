# Citation Rules

**Purpose**: Standardizing how Regulayer is cited in formal documents.

## Academic & Research
> "Integrity verification performed using the Regulayer Evidence Standard [Version]."
> *Cite specific Snapshot IDs used in datasets.*

## Regulatory Filings
> "System logs are anchored using a hash-chain implementation compatible with Regulayer v1.0. Verification proofs are attached as Exhibit B."

## Marketing (Restricted)
> "Secured by Regulayer" - **PERMITTED**
> "Regulayer Certified" - **FORBIDDEN**
> "Auditable via Regulayer" - **PERMITTED**
> "Regulayer Approved" - **FORBIDDEN**
