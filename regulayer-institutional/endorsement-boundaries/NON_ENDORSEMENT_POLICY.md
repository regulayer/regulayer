# Non-Endorsement Policy

**Effective Date**: Immediate
**Scope**: All users, partners, and implementers of Regulayer.

## The Core Rule
Regulayer **verified** means "Authentic and Unaltered".
Regulayer **verified** does NOT mean "Safe", "Fair", "Correct", or "Legal".

## Prohibited Claims
Users strictly **MUST NOT** verify, imply, or market that:
- "Regulayer certifies this AI is safe."
- "This model is Regulayer Approved."
- "Regulayer guarantees compliance with [Law/Regulation]."

## Vendor Neutrality
Regulayer Inc. maintains strict neutrality regarding the *content* of verified decisions. We provide the seal of integrity, not the seal of approval.
