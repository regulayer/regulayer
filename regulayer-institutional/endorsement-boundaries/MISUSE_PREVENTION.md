# Misuse Prevention Policy

**Purpose**: Preventing the weaponization of trust.

## Revocation of Trust
Regulayer reserves the right (via Trusted Key Lists or Public Revocation) to flag organizations that:
1. Systematically misrepresent the meaning of verification.
2. Use verification to cloak malicious behavior (e.g., verifying "valid" logs of a scam).

## Public Correction
If a high-profile entity misuses Regulayer to claim false safety (e.g., "Our AI isn't biased because it's Regulayer verified"), Regulayer Inc. commits to issuing a **Public Correction** clarifying that verification only covers data integrity, not bias.

## Contractual Enforcement
Enterprise contracts include immediate termination clauses for "Trust Washing" (using the brand to sanitize unethical conduct).
