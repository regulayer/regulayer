# Jurisdiction Notes

## GDPR (EU)
- **Right to Erasure**: Regulayer hashes are irreversible. Deleting the source record renders the hash meaningless (Crypto-Shredding). We support "Forgettable" proofs.
- **Data Processor**: Regulayer acts as a Processor.

## United States
- **Federal Rules of Evidence**: Regulayer proofs satisfy FRE 902(13) and 902(14) for self-authenticating electronic records.

## India
- **IT Act 2000**: Regulayer signatures qualify as Secure Electronic Records when linked to recognized certifying authorities (if configured).
