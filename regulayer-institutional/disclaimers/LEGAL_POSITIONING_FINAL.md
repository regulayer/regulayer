# Legal Positioning (Final)

**The Authoritative Statement of Regulayer's Role**

Regulayer provides **cryptographic evidence of recorded events**.
It does **not** regulate, approve, certify, or judge AI systems or their outputs.

## For Courts and Regulators
Regulayer is a **Chain of Custody** tool. It ensures that the digital evidence presented in court is the same bits-and-bytes that were generated by the AI system at the time of the event.

## For Disputes
Regulayer Inc. is **not an arbiter**. We provide the mathematical tools for parties to resolve their own disputes regarding data integrity.

## Universality
This position applies globally, across all jurisdictions. No local sales team or partner represents Regulayer as having authority beyond this definition.
