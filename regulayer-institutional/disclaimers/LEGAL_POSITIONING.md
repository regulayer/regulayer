# Legal Positioning

**For General Counsel and Legal Teams**

## What is Regulayer?
Legally, Regulayer is a **Digital Signature and Time-Stamping Service**.
Under eIDAS (EU) and ESIGN (US), it generates **Advanced Electronic Signatures**.

## Liability
Regulayer Inc. warrants the correct functioning of the cryptographic verification math.
Regulayer Inc. explicitly disclaims liability for the *decisions* made by systems using Regulayer.

## Discovery
Regulayer Inc. cannot decrypt customer data encrypted with customer keys. We cannot respond to subpoenas for record content we do not possess.
