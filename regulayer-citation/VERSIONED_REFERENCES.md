# Versioned References

**Stopping "Method Drift" via Version Pinning**

Citations must be pinned to a specific version of the standard. Citations of "Regulayer" in the abstract are considered **void**.

## Why Versioning Matters
- **v1.0**: Uses SHA-256 / Ed25519.
- **v2.0 (Hypothetical)**: Might use SHA-3 / Dilithium (Post-Quantum).

A claim valid under v1 might be invalid under v2.

## Citation Format
You MUST cite: `Regulayer Evidence Standard [Version]`.
- **Good**: "Verified under Regulayer v1.2."
- **Bad**: "Verified by Regulayer."

## Deprecation
Citations of deprecated versions (e.g., v0.9-beta) are considered **Historical Only** and carry no current weight of trust.
