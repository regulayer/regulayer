# Quotation Rules

**Preserving the Integrity of Definitions**

When quoting Regulayer definitions in formal documents, you must adhere to these rules to prevent "context collapse."

## 1. No Partial Quotation of "VALID"
You may not quote the definition of `VALID` without including the scope limitation.
- **Allowed**: "VALID means the record is authentic and unaltered."
- **Forbidden**: "VALID means the record is authentic..." (leaving out "unaltered" or context).

## 2. Mandatory Context for "Verified"
Every use of the word "Verified" in a summary must be qualified by the *object* of verification.
- **Allowed**: "Verified Record."
- **Allowed**: "Verified Integrity."
- **Forbidden**: "Verified System." (Ambiguous - implies the whole system is verified).

## 3. The "Not Safe" Disclaimer
If you quote our claims of Integrity, you are **encouraged** (and for Partners, **required**) to footnote the "Not AI Safety" disclaimer.
