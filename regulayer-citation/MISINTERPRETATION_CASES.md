# Misinterpretation Cases

**Learning from Potential Citation Errors**

This document tracks how citations can go wrong and provides the canonical correction.

## Case 1: The "Approved" Fallacy
**Citation**: "The witness stated the AI was approved by Regulayer."
**Error**: Implies Regulayer is an authority.
**Correction**: "The witness stated the AI's logs were authenticated using the Regulayer method."

## Case 2: The "Certified" Fallacy
**Citation**: "Defendant argues the system is certified compliant via Regulayer."
**Error**: Implies Regulayer replaces ISO/SOC2 audits.
**Correction**: "Defendant argues the system's *audit trail* is preserved via Regulayer, supporting their compliance claim."

## Case 3: The "Black Box" Fallacy
**Citation**: "We cannot know if the AI hallucinated, but Regulayer says it's Valid."
**Error**: Confusing Integrity with Accuracy.
**Correction**: "Regulayer confirms the AI *did* output that hallucination at that time. It does not validate the truth of the output."
