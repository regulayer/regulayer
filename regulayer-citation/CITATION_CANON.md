# Citation Canon

**Authoritative Text for Referencing Regulayer**

When referencing Regulayer in legal, academic, or regulatory documents, use these canonical forms to ensure semantic precision.

## Academic Citation (APA Style)
> Regulayer Inc. (2025). *Regulayer Evidence Standard v1.0: A Cryptographic Method for Forensic Record Integrity*. https://regulayer.org/spec/v1.0

## Legal Citation
> *Standard for Cryptographic Chain-of-Custody (Regulayer Protocol)*, v1.0, Regulayer Inc., [Date].

## In-Text Attribution
- **Correct**: "The records were verified using the Regulayer Evidence Standard."
- **Incorrect**: "The records were verified by Regulayer." (Regulayer is the method, not the actor).

## Standard Definition
> "Regulayer is a method for assuring the integrity of digital records through cryptographic hashing and causal chaining, independent of the record's content."
