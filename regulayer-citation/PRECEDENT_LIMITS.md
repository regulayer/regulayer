# Precedent Limits

**Strict Boundaries for Judicial Interpretation**

To prevent "Interpretive Drift," Regulayer explicitly limits the scope of judicial precedent derived from its use.

## The Non-Expansion Clause
A court ruling that accepts Regulayer evidence as **Authentic** does NOT create a precedent that Regulayer evidence is **Sufficient** for:
- Proving Intent.
- Proving Fairness.
- Proving Regulatory Compliance.

## Precedent Isolation
If a court rules that "Regulayer verification implies Safety" in a specific case, Regulayer Inc. formally rejects this interpretation as a **Misuse of Method**.
- **Reason**: Courts define law, but they cannot redefine math. If the math does not prove safety, a judge saying it does is technically incorrect.

## Authorized Precedent
The only precedent we accept and support is:
> "Regulayer verification is sufficient to establish that a digital record has not been altered since the timestamped moment of recording."
