# EU AI Act — Evidence Support Mapping

Version: 1.0.0  
Framework: EU Artificial Intelligence Act (2024)  
Document Type: Evidence Alignment (NOT Compliance Claim)

---

## Framework Overview

The EU AI Act establishes requirements for high-risk AI systems. This document maps Regulayer evidence capabilities to relevant EU AI Act obligations.

⚠️ **Critical Note:** This mapping focuses on Article 9-15 obligations for high-risk systems.

---

## Article 9 — Risk Management System

**What the standard asks:**  
High-risk AI systems shall have a risk management system.

**What Regulayer provides:**
- ✅ Complete decision record history
- ✅ Incident tracking and disclosure
- ✅ Trust degradation analysis

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ Risk identification methodology
- ❌ Risk assessment scoring
- ❌ Risk mitigation planning

**Evidence artifacts:**
- Incident registry
- Disclosure documents
- Trust status reports

---

## Article 10 — Data and Data Governance

**What the standard asks:**  
Training, validation, and testing datasets shall be subject to appropriate governance.

**What Regulayer provides:**
- Decision record preservation (not training data)
- Immutable audit trail of decisions

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ Training data management
- ❌ Data quality assessment
- ❌ Dataset bias evaluation

⚠️ **Note:** Regulayer records AI decisions, not AI training data.

---

## Article 11 — Technical Documentation

**What the standard asks:**  
Technical documentation shall be drawn up before the system is placed on the market.

**What Regulayer provides:**
- ✅ System trust reports documenting architecture
- ✅ Cryptographic specifications
- ✅ API documentation

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ AI model documentation
- ❌ Design justification
- ❌ Conformity assessment documentation

---

## Article 12 — Record-Keeping ⭐

**What the standard asks:**  
High-risk AI systems shall be designed to automatically record events (logs).

**What Regulayer provides:**
- ✅ FULL SUPPORT
- ✅ Immutable decision records
- ✅ Timestamp preservation
- ✅ Identity attribution
- ✅ Chain integrity verification
- ✅ Offline verification capability
- ✅ 10+ year archival support

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ Model inference logs (input/output tensors)

**Evidence artifacts:**
- Complete proof bundle exports
- Submission packages
- Chain integrity reports

---

## Article 14 — Human Oversight ⭐

**What the standard asks:**  
High-risk AI systems shall be designed to allow human oversight.

**What Regulayer provides:**
- ✅ Governance layer for human review
- ✅ Approval workflows
- ✅ Segregation of duties (analyst cannot approve)
- ✅ Audit trail of human actions
- ✅ Review state management

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ Override mechanisms for AI decisions
- ❌ Real-time intervention capability

**Evidence artifacts:**
- Governance evidence exports
- Approval records
- Access control logs

---

## Article 15 — Accuracy, Robustness, Cybersecurity

**What the standard asks:**  
High-risk AI systems shall achieve appropriate levels of accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity.

**What Regulayer provides:**
- ✅ Record integrity (SHA-256 hash chain)
- ✅ Cryptographic signing (Ed25519)
- ✅ Tamper detection
- ✅ Key rotation without history break

**What Regulayer does NOT provide:**
- ❌ AI accuracy measurement
- ❌ AI robustness testing
- ❌ Bias or fairness evaluation

**Evidence artifacts:**
- Proof bundles
- Cryptographic snapshots
- Key rotation logs
