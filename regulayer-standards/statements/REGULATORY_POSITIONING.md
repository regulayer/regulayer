# Regulatory Positioning Statement

Version: 1.0.0  
Document Type: External Communication Framework

---

## Purpose

This document defines how Regulayer should be described to regulators, courts, and external parties.

---

## Approved Descriptions

### Primary Description

> **"Regulayer is evidence infrastructure for AI decision accountability."**

### Alternative Approved Descriptions

- "Cryptographic audit layer for AI systems"
- "Forensic accountability infrastructure"
- "Immutable decision recording system"
- "Hash-chain evidence infrastructure"

---

## Category Positioning

### What Regulayer IS

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Evidence Infrastructure** | Records AI decisions with cryptographic integrity |
| **Forensic Audit Layer** | Enables independent verification of decision history |
| **Accountability System** | Provides identity-attested, immutable records |
| **Record-Keeping Solution** | Meets Article 12 (EU AI Act) record-keeping needs |

### What Regulayer is NOT

| Category | Why Not |
|----------|---------|
| **AI Safety System** | Does not evaluate or improve AI safety |
| **Compliance Solution** | Does not certify compliance with any regulation |
| **Ethics Platform** | Does not assess ethical implications |
| **Bias Detection Tool** | Does not measure bias or fairness |
| **Risk Assessment Engine** | Does not score or evaluate risks |
| **Model Validation System** | Does not validate AI model correctness |

---

## Prohibited Claims

Never state or imply:

❌ "Regulayer ensures AI compliance"
❌ "Regulayer makes AI safe"
❌ "Regulayer prevents AI bias"
❌ "Regulayer certifies AI decisions"
❌ "Regulayer validates AI outputs"

✅ "Regulayer records AI decisions immutably"

## Regulatory Context Statements

### When Asked "Does Regulayer ensure compliance?"

> "Regulayer provides verifiable evidence that may support compliance programs. 
> It does not certify compliance or replace comprehensive compliance programs."

### When Asked "Does Regulayer make AI safe?"

> "Regulayer records AI decisions and proves they weren't altered. 
> It does not evaluate AI safety, fairness, or correctness."

### When Asked "What regulations does Regulayer comply with?"

> "Regulayer is designed to provide evidence that may support various regulatory requirements, 
> including EU AI Act Article 12 (record-keeping) and Article 14 (human oversight). 
> Specific compliance determinations should be made by qualified assessors."

---

## Court Context Statements

### When Asked "What does this system prove?"

> "Regulayer proves that AI decisions were recorded and not altered.
> It proves who authorized decisions and when.
> It does not prove that decisions were correct, fair, or legal."

### When Asked "Can I trust this system?"

> "You don't need to trust Regulayer. 
> You can independently verify all evidence using standard cryptography.
> The verification tools are open source and auditable."

## Stakeholder-Specific Positioning

### For Regulators
Emphasis: Record-keeping, audit trails, independent verification

### For Legal Teams
Emphasis: Evidence integrity, non-repudiation, offline verification

### For Auditors
Emphasis: Complete records, deterministic verification, tool availability

### For Enterprises
Emphasis: EU AI Act support, governance workflows, procurement readiness
