# Non-Compliance Statement

Version: 1.0.0  
Document Type: Legal Protection Document

---

## Purpose

This document explicitly states what Regulayer does NOT do. This protects against regulatory overreach and sets clear expectations.

---

## Explicit Non-Claims

### Regulayer is NOT a Risk Engine

Regulayer does not:
- Identify AI risks
- Assess risk probability
- Score risk severity
- Prioritize risk mitigation
- Recommend risk responses

**What it does instead:**
Records incidents and tracks trust degradation when risks materialize.

---

### Regulayer is NOT a Bias Auditor

Regulayer does not:
- Detect bias in AI systems
- Measure fairness metrics
- Evaluate protected class impacts
- Recommend debiasing approaches
- Certify bias-free operation

**What it does instead:**
Records AI decisions for later analysis by qualified bias auditors.

---

### Regulayer is NOT a Correctness Validator

Regulayer does not:
- Evaluate whether AI decisions are correct
- Compare AI outputs to ground truth
- Measure accuracy or precision
- Validate business appropriateness
- Guarantee decision quality

**What it does instead:**
Proves that recorded decisions were not altered after recording.

---

### Regulayer is NOT a Compliance Program

Regulayer does not:
- Certify compliance with any regulation
- Replace compliance assessments
- Provide legal opinions
- Guarantee regulatory approval
- Substitute for legal counsel

**What it does instead:**
Provides evidence that may support compliance programs.

---

### Regulayer is NOT an AI Safety System

Regulayer does not:
- Prevent harmful AI decisions
- Stop AI from making errors
- Ensure AI aligns with human values
- Guarantee safe AI operation
- Monitor AI behavior in real-time

**What it does instead:**
Creates an immutable record of AI decisions for accountability.

---

## Boundary Statement

> **Regulayer provides cryptographic evidence infrastructure.**
> 
> It proves records exist and weren't changed.
> It proves who authorized records and when.
> 
> **It does not evaluate, validate, certify, or guarantee**
> **the correctness, fairness, safety, or legality**
> **of AI decisions.**

---

## Legal Protection Language

This statement should be included in all contracts and agreements:

```
LIMITATION OF SCOPE

Regulayer provides evidence recording and verification services only.
Customer acknowledges that Regulayer does not:

(a) Evaluate the correctness of AI decisions
(b) Assess bias or fairness of AI systems
(c) Certify compliance with any regulation
(d) Replace comprehensive risk management programs
(e) Guarantee AI safety or ethical operation

Customer is solely responsible for AI governance, compliance, 
and risk management beyond record integrity verification.
```

---

## Frequently Misunderstood Boundaries

### "Regulayer passed verification, so the AI decision was correct"
**Wrong.** Verification proves the record wasn't altered. It says nothing about correctness.

### "Regulayer has no incidents, so the AI is safe"
**Wrong.** Regulayer tracks recording integrity incidents, not AI safety issues.

### "Regulayer approved the decision"
**Wrong.** Governance approvals are organizational process, not Regulayer endorsement.

### "Regulayer certifies EU AI Act compliance"
**Wrong.** Regulayer provides evidence that may support compliance. It does not certify.
