# Community Participation

**How the Ecosystem Participates**

## Model: Advisory Democracy
Regulayer's governance listens to the community but protects the core invariants against "Mob Rule".

## Roles
- **Maintainers**: Commit access to the repo. Must enforce the Charter.
- **Contributors**: Submit PRs and RFCs.
- **Partners**: Run Time Anchors and Mirrors.
- **Users**: Hold the ultimate power—the power to verify (or not verify) using our tools.

## Preventing Democratic Capture
No community vote can force the Governance Body to violate the Charter (e.g., voting to Retroactively Invalidate a competitor's proofs). The Charter is superior to the Vote.
