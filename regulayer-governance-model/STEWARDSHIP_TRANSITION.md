# Stewardship Transition

**Passing the Torch**

## Trigger Conditions
If Regulayer Inc. (the Vendor) dissolves, abandons the project, or acts maliciously (violating the Charter).

## Transition Plan
1.  **Escrow Release**: Verified binaries and docs move to Public Domain (see Escrow Model).
2.  **Trust Registry Transfer**: Control of the `Trusted Key List` moves to a pre-selected Non-Profit Foundation (e.g., Linux Foundation or newly formed DAO).
3.  **Root Key Sunset**: The Vendor Root Key is sunsetted. New Root Keys are issued by the Foundation.

## User Impact
- **Historical Proofs**: Remain valid (checked against old Vendor Key).
- **New Proofs**: Signed by new Foundation Keys.
- **Service**: Users migrate to self-hosted or community-hosted nodes.

**Guarantee**: No single individual or company is required for Regulayer to remain valid.
