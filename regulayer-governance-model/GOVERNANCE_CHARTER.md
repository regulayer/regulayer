# Governance Charter

**Purpose**: This Charter defines the immutable purpose of Regulayer governance.

## Mission Statement
The purpose of Regulayer governance is to **preserve the immutability, verifiability, and interpretability of cryptographic evidence across time, vendors, and jurisdictions.**

## Explicit Non-Purposes
Governance exists to protect the method, not to police the content. Regulayer Governance explicitly **DOES NOT**:
1.  **Certify Systems**: We do not issue "Safety" or "Fairness" certificates.
2.  **Approve Vendors**: We do not pick winners or losers in the AI market.
3.  **Enforce Compliance**: We provide tools for regulators, we do not act as a regulator.
4.  **Judge Correctness**: We do not arbitrate whether an AI's decision was "right".
5.  **Control Users**: We do not censor who can use the open-source recorder. (We may revoke keys of bad actors from the *Official Trust Registry*, but we cannot stop them from forking).

## Primacy of Math
In any conflict between "Governance Authority" and "Cryptographic Truth", the Cryptographic Truth prevails. Governance cannot vote 2+2=5.
