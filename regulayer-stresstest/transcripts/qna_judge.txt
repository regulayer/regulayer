REGULAYER Q&A TRANSCRIPT: COURTROOM EXAMINATION
================================================

This document contains plain-English answers for judges and juries.
No technical jargon. Written for legal audiences.

---

JUDGE: "Explain to the court what this Regulayer system does."

WITNESS: "Your Honor, Regulayer is like a notary for AI decisions. When an AI 
system makes a decision, we create a permanent record of exactly what it 
decided. This record cannot be changed later without detection.

Think of it as a tamper-evident lockbox. Anyone can verify the seal wasn't 
broken, even years later."

---

JUDGE: "How can I trust that this record wasn't altered?"

WITNESS: "Your Honor, each record has a unique digital fingerprint - like a 
DNA sequence. This fingerprint is mathematically calculated from the record's 
contents.

If even a single character was changed, the fingerprint would be completely 
different. You can calculate this fingerprint yourself using standard software 
that is publicly available and compare it to what we stored.

Furthermore, each record's fingerprint includes the previous record's 
fingerprint. They form an unbreakable chain. Changing any record breaks the 
entire chain from that point forward."

---

JUDGE: "I don't understand computers. How do I verify this independently?"

WITNESS: "Your Honor, you don't need to understand computers to verify this. 
Here's what you can do:

1. Appoint a technical expert of your choosing
2. Give them the evidence package - it's a standard zip file
3. Give them the verification software - it's publicly available
4. Have them run the verification process
5. They will confirm whether the records are intact

The expert doesn't need to contact us or use our systems. Everything needed 
for verification is in the package."

---

ATTORNEY: "How do we know the company didn't pre-compute these fingerprints?"

WITNESS: "That's mathematically impossible. Let me explain:

Each new fingerprint includes the previous fingerprint. To pre-compute future 
fingerprints, you would need to know what decisions the AI would make in the 
future. That's impossible.

It's like asking someone to forge tomorrow's newspaper headline. They can't 
know what news will happen."

---

ATTORNEY: "What if the signing key was stolen?"

WITNESS: "If a key is compromised, we take these steps:

1. We revoke the key and issue a new one
2. We record when the compromise was discovered
3. Records signed BEFORE the compromise remain valid
4. Only records signed DURING the compromise period are questioned

We don't retroactively invalidate all historical records. Each record is 
evaluated based on when it was created relative to any incident."

---

JUDGE: "The defendant claims this record was fabricated. How do you respond?"

WITNESS: "Your Honor, fabrication would require the defendant to:

1. Create a fake record
2. Compute a fingerprint that matches all subsequent records
3. Insert it into the chain without breaking the chain
4. Forge a digital signature from an authorized identity

This is mathematically impossible. The chain would immediately show the break. 
The verification tool would report failure."

---

ATTORNEY: "Does this system prove the AI made the right decision?"

WITNESS: "No. And we never claim it does.

Regulayer proves that a decision was recorded and the record wasn't changed. 
It does not prove:

- The decision was correct
- The AI was fair
- The outcome was legal

Think of it like a security camera. The camera proves what happened. It 
doesn't prove whether what happened was right or wrong."

---

JUDGE: "What are the limitations of this evidence?"

WITNESS: "Your Honor, I want to be completely clear about our limitations:

1. We prove records exist and weren't changed
2. We prove who signed records and when
3. We do NOT prove AI correctness or fairness
4. We do NOT guarantee compliance with any law
5. We do NOT evaluate the quality of AI decisions

Our evidence is like certified mail receipt. It proves delivery. It doesn't 
prove the contents of the letter were true."

---

ATTORNEY: "Can you produce all records for the relevant time period?"

WITNESS: "Yes. We have prepared a complete evidence package containing:

1. All decision records from the requested period
2. A master list of all fingerprints (called a manifest)
3. Verification that no records are missing
4. Chain integrity report showing the sequence is complete

Every record can be independently verified using the tools we provide."
