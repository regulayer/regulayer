# Press Reference Text

**For Media Inquiries and Articles**

## Boilerplate
> "Regulayer is a cryptographic transparency standard that allows organizations to mathematically prove their data has not been tampered with. It acts as a digital witness for algorithmic decisions."

## Correction Guidance
If a reporter writes: "Regulayer certifies that the AI is safe."
**Correction**: "Regulayer certifies that the AI's logs are authentic. It does not evaluate safety."

## Headline Examples (Approved)
- "Bank Uses Regulayer to Lock Down AI Audit Trails."
- "Regulayer Verification Proves Records Were Not Altered."

## Headline Examples (Incorrect)
- "Regulayer Approves New AI Model." (We don't approve).
- "Regulayer Says AI is Legal." (We aren't lawyers).
