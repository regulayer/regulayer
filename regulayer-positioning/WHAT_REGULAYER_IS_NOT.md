# What Regulayer Is NOT

**Explicit Negatives**

To prevent misuse, over-reliance, and legal overreach, Regulayer is explicitly defined by what it is **NOT**.

## 1. Compliance Certification
❌ Regulayer is **NOT** a compliance certifier. verification does not shield a user from liability under the EU AI Act, GDPR, or other regulations.

## 2. AI Evaluation
❌ Regulayer is **NOT** an AI evaluator. It does not test for bias, fairness, accuracy, or hallucinations. It only proves the output was recorded as-is.

## 3. Decision Arbiter
❌ Regulayer is **NOT** a judge. It does not decide if a loan denial was "Legal" or "Ethical". It only proves the denial record existed at Time T.

## 4. Correctness Guarantee
❌ Regulayer is **NOT** a guarantee of correctness. A verified record of a "wrong" decision is still a verified record. Garbage In, Verified Garbage Out.
