# What Regulayer Is

**Canonical Definition**

Regulayer is a **Cryptographic Evidence Recording and Verification Method**.

## Functional Scope
1.  **Tamper Detection**: It detects if a digital record has been modified since the moment of recording.
2.  **Attribution**: It cryptographically links a record to a specific signing identity (Key).
3.  **Sequencing**: It orders records in a tamper-evident causal chain.
4.  **Forensics**: It provides a method to audit the history of a system post-hoc.

## Infrastructure Role
Regulayer functions as **Digital Trust Infrastructure**. It is akin to a "Notary Public" for algorithmic decisions—it witnesses the signing of the document but does not read or vouch for its contents.
