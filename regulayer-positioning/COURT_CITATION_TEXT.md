## Admissibility Statement
> "The digital evidence attached as Exhibit A consists of cryptographic records generated in accordance with the Regulayer Evidence Standard v1.0. The integrity of these records has been verified using the open-source reference verifier, confirming that the data has not been altered since its creation on [Date]."

## Expert Witness Statement
> "I have independently verified the cryptographic signatures associated with these records. Based on the Ed25519 signature scheme and SHA-256 hashing algorithm, it is my professional opinion that these records originate from the entity holding the Key ID [KeyID] and have maintained their integrity."
