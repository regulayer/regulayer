# Delegation Rules

**Rules for Partners, Resellers, and Auditors**

## 1. No "Certified Partners"
We do not certify partners. We have "Authorized Resellers" (who sell licenses) and "Integration Partners" (who build adapters).
- **Correct**: "We are an Authorized Reseller of Regulayer."
- **Incorrect**: "We are a Regulayer Certified Auditor." (Regulayer does not certify auditors).

## 2. No Value-Add on Trust
Partners cannot "Add" trust to the protocol. They can only "expose" the trust that exists.
- **Forbidden**: "Our dashboard makes Regulayer verification more secure." (False. The math is the security).

## 3. Disclaimers Travel Downstream
Partners reselling Regulayer MUST include the [Institutional Disclaimer](../regulayer-institutional/disclaimers/INSTITUTIONAL_DISCLAIMER.md) in their own end-user contracts.
