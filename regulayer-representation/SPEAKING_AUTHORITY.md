# Speaking Authority

**Who Speaks for Regulayer?**

## The Core Principle
Regulayer is a verification protocol (The Method). No human being speaks for the mathematical results of the Protocol.

## Authority Matrix

| Context | Who Speaks? | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Verification Result** | The Verifier Tool | "Valid/Invalid" only. |
| **Protocol Specs** | The Governance Body | Changes to V2+. |
| **Commercials** | Regulayer Inc. | Pricing, SLAs. |
| **Interpretation** | The User | What the evidence means for their business. |
| **Correctness** | **NO ONE** | No one has authority to say "Regulayer confirms this decision was right." |

## Negative Authority Prohibition
**Mandatory Rule**:
No individual, company, foundation, or successor entity may claim authority to reinterpret verification results, redefine validity semantics, or override cryptographic outcomes.
- We cannot "Unverify" a valid proof.
- We cannot "Deem Valid" an invalid proof.
The math is sovereign.

## Silence by Default
If a question falls outside the defined scopes, the official stance of Regulayer is **Silence**. We do not offer opinions on AI ethics, politics, or market trends.
