# Evidence Limitations

**What Regulayer Verification Does NOT Prove**

1.  **Input Quality**: We prove the output was recorded as generated. We do not prove the input data was accurate ("Garbage In, Verified Garbage Out").
2.  **Model Logic**: We do not validate the internal weights or logic of the AI model.
3.  **Human Intent**: We do not prove the intent of the operator.
4.  **Legal Compliance**: Verification of a process does not automatically make that process legal in any specific jurisdiction.

This document refers to the **Integrity of the Record**, not the **Quality of the Decision**.
