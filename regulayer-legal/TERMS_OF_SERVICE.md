# Regulayer Terms of Service

**Effective Date: January 2026**

## 1. Service Description

Regulayer provides decision record infrastructure for AI systems. We record, hash, chain, and attest decisions submitted via our API. 

We are a **logging and auditing** service. We do not:
- Make decisions on your behalf
- Evaluate AI correctness
- Certify compliance
- Provide legal advice

## 2. What We Guarantee

- **Immutability**: Recorded decisions cannot be modified
- **Verifiability**: Proofs are independently verifiable offline
- **Availability**: Per our SLA (99.9% for paid plans)
- **Isolation**: Your data is isolated from other organizations

## 3. What We Do NOT Guarantee

- That your AI decisions are correct
- That recording decisions makes you compliant
- That proofs will be accepted by any particular regulator
- Uptime beyond SLA commitments

## 4. Your Responsibilities

- Maintain API key security
- Submit decisions in good faith
- Not exceed rate limits maliciously
- Comply with applicable laws

## 5. Billing

- Free tier: Limited quota, no payment required
- Paid tiers: Monthly billing via Stripe
- Non-payment: Ingestion suspended, exports remain available
- Cancellation: Proofs remain valid and exportable

## 6. Data Retention

- Decision records: Retained per your plan
- Proofs: Exportable at any time
- After termination: 30-day export window, then deletion

## 7. Trust Boundaries

Regulayer records facts. We do not:
- Inspect payload semantics
- Make judgments about content
- Modify submitted data

## 8. Limitation of Liability

Regulayer's liability is limited to fees paid in the affected period. We are not liable for decisions made using AI systems that use our service.

## 9. Changes

We will notify users 30 days before material changes to these terms.
