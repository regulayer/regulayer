# Regulayer Launch Trust Guardrails

## Why This Exists

This folder contains the trust-safe language standards that govern ALL public-facing communication from Regulayer.

**Violations are trust incidents, not "marketing mistakes."**

---

## Who Must Follow This

- **Everyone.**
- Marketing
- Sales
- Engineering (docs, comments)
- Support
- Leadership (press, board)
- Partners

---

## Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `PUBLIC_CLAIMS.md` | What we ARE allowed to claim |
| `PROHIBITED_CLAIMS.md` | What we are NEVER allowed to claim |
| `TRUST_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md` | Canonical vocabulary and phrasing |
| `UI_COPY_RULES.md` | Rules for all visible UI text |
| `SALES_SAFE_PHRASES.md` | What sales can say |
| `PRESS_QA.md` | Prepared answers for media |

---

## Core Principle

> **Be boringly precise. That's what regulators trust.**

### We Say

- Verifiable
- Detectable  
- Supports
- Provides
- Records

### We Never Say

- Ensures
- Guarantees
- Certifies
- Approves
- Prevents

---

## Quick Reference

### Allowed

✅ "Cryptographically verifiable records"
✅ "Tamper-evident evidence"
✅ "Supports audit workflows"
✅ "Independently verifiable"
✅ "Proof bundles you can verify offline"

### Prohibited

❌ "Ensures compliance"
❌ "Guarantees legal protection"
❌ "Certifies AI systems"
❌ "Prevents fraud"
❌ "Approved decisions"

## Violation Response

| Severity | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Critical | Immediate removal, incident report |
| High | Same-day correction |
| Medium | Scheduled fix |
| Low | Coaching |

## Why This Matters

Without these guardrails:

- A single blog post can destroy regulator trust
- A sales call can create legal exposure
- A UI label can invalidate courtroom credibility

With these guardrails:

- Regulayer becomes boringly precise
- That's exactly what regulators trust
