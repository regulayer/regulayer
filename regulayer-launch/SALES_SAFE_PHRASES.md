# Sales Safe Phrases

## Purpose

This document defines what sales can say without legal risk.
These phrases have been reviewed for accuracy and safety.

---

## Safe Elevator Pitches

### 30-Second Version

> "Regulayer creates cryptographic records of AI decisions.
> Every record is signed, chained, and independently verifiable.
> If there's ever a question about what happened, you have proof."

### Technical Version

> "Regulayer is an append-only evidence layer for AI systems.
> Every decision is hashed, signed, and linked in a chain.
> Proofs can be verified offline without us.
> It's built for regulators, auditors, and courts."

### Enterprise Version

> "Regulayer gives you auditable evidence of AI decision-making.
> It supports compliance workflows without claiming to make you compliant.
> The proofs survive even if Regulayer doesn't."

## Safe Enterprise Positioning

### To CTOs/CISOs

> "Regulayer provides verifiable evidence of AI decisions.
> It's designed for high-stakes environments where you need to prove
> what your AI did, when, and that the record hasn't been tampered with."

### To Legal/Compliance

> "Regulayer supports audit and regulatory workflows.
> It doesn't determine compliance—that's your lawyers' job.
> It provides the evidence trail they'll need."

### To Risk Officers

> "Regulayer creates evidence that's independently verifiable.
> If a regulator asks what happened, you have cryptographic proof.
> The records are immutable and the verification is offline."

## Common Questions & Safe Answers

### "Does this make us compliant?"

❌ NEVER say: "Yes, this makes you compliant."

✅ SAFE answer:
> "Regulayer doesn't make compliance determinations—that's for your legal team.
> What we do is provide evidence that supports compliance workflows.
> You'll have documentation that auditors and regulators can verify."

### "Can regulators rely on this?"

❌ NEVER say: "Regulators will accept this."

✅ SAFE answer:
> "The evidence is independently verifiable.
> Regulators can check it themselves without trusting us.
> What they conclude from the evidence is their determination."

### "What if Regulayer shuts down?"

✅ SAFE answer:
> "Proofs you've exported remain valid forever.
> Verification is offline—it doesn't require our systems.
> Even if we disappeared tomorrow, your evidence still verifies."

### "Is this like blockchain?"

✅ SAFE answer:
> "It uses similar cryptographic techniques—hashing and signatures.
> But it's not a blockchain. There's no cryptocurrency or consensus mechanism.
> It's a straightforward append-only chain with attestations."

### "Does this prove our AI is fair/unbiased?"

❌ NEVER say: "Yes, it proves fairness."

✅ SAFE answer:
> "Regulayer doesn't assess fairness—that's a separate analysis.
> What it does is record decisions so fairness can be audited.
> You'll have the evidence trail needed for bias audits."

### "Will this satisfy the EU AI Act?"

❌ NEVER say: "Yes, you'll be AI Act compliant."

✅ SAFE answer:
> "Regulayer supports AI Act documentation requirements.
> Whether you satisfy the AI Act is a legal determination.
> We provide evidence that supports that documentation."

### "Do you certify our AI system?"

❌ NEVER say: "Yes" or imply any certification.

✅ SAFE answer:
> "We don't certify anything. We record and attest.
> Certification is what auditors do with the evidence we provide."

## Phrases to Use

| Context | Safe Phrase |
|---------|-------------|
| General | "Supports your compliance workflows" |
| Technical | "Independently verifiable evidence" |
| Regulatory | "Documentation for regulatory review" |
| Legal | "Evidence trail for audits and disputes" |
| Enterprise | "Cryptographic records of AI decisions" |

---

## Phrases to NEVER Use

| ❌ Never | Why |
|---------|-----|
| "This makes you compliant" | Legal determination |
| "Regulators will approve this" | We don't speak for regulators |
| "This certifies your AI" | We don't certify |
| "This prevents lawsuits" | Cannot guarantee legal outcomes |
| "You're covered" | Misleading |
| "You're protected" | Misleading |

## Handling Pushback

### "I need something that makes us compliant."

> "I understand. Compliance itself is a legal determination based on many factors.
> Regulayer provides one piece: verifiable evidence of AI decisions.
> Your legal team uses that evidence to demonstrate compliance."

### "Can you put in writing that this satisfies [X regulation]?"

> "We can document exactly what Regulayer provides.
> We can't make legal determinations about regulations—that's outside our expertise.
> We're happy to work with your legal team to explain our capabilities."
