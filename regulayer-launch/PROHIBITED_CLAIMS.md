# Prohibited Claims

## Purpose

This document defines what Regulayer is NEVER allowed to claim.
These are hard red lines. Violations are trust incidents, not marketing mistakes.

---

## ❌ PROHIBITED CLAIMS

### Compliance Claims

| Prohibited | Why Dangerous | Say Instead |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ "Regulayer ensures AI compliance" | Implies legal guarantee | ✅ "Regulayer supports compliance workflows" |
| ❌ "Regulayer guarantees legal compliance" | Legal liability | ✅ "Regulayer provides evidence for compliance assessment" |
| ❌ "AI Act compliant" | Compliance is regulator's determination | ✅ "Supports AI Act audit workflows" |
| ❌ "GDPR compliant decisions" | Misrepresents scope | ✅ "Supports GDPR documentation requirements" |
| ❌ "SOC2 certified" | Only applies to our operations | ✅ "SOC2-auditable evidence exports" |

### Correctness Claims

| Prohibited | Why Dangerous | Say Instead |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ "Regulayer proves AI decisions are correct" | We prove recording, not correctness | ✅ "Regulayer proves decisions were recorded" |
| ❌ "Verified AI decisions" | Implies decision quality | ✅ "Cryptographically verified records" |
| ❌ "Trustworthy AI" | Trust is about verifiability | ✅ "Verifiable AI decision records" |
| ❌ "Safe AI decisions" | We don't assess safety | ✅ "Auditable AI decision records" |

### Prevention Claims

| Prohibited | Why Dangerous | Say Instead |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ "Regulayer prevents fraud" | We detect, not prevent | ✅ "Regulayer makes tampering detectable" |
| ❌ "Regulayer prevents bias" | We record, not fix | ✅ "Regulayer records decisions for bias audits" |
| ❌ "Regulayer stops bad AI" | Beyond our scope | ✅ "Regulayer provides evidence for AI oversight" |
| ❌ "Regulayer ensures fairness" | We don't assess fairness | ✅ "Regulayer supports fairness audits" |

### Authority Claims

| Prohibited | Why Dangerous | Say Instead |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ "Regulayer-approved decisions" | We never approve | ✅ "Regulayer-recorded decisions" |
| ❌ "Regulayer-certified AI" | We never certify | ✅ "Regulayer-attestations included" |
| ❌ "Regulayer validates AI models" | We don't validate models | ✅ "Regulayer records model decisions" |
| ❌ "Regulayer audits AI systems" | Audits are third-party | ✅ "Regulayer supports AI audits" |

### Blockchain/Crypto Hype

| Prohibited | Why Dangerous | Say Instead |
|------------|---------------|-------------|
| ❌ "Blockchain-based" | Technically incorrect | ✅ "Hash-chained" |
| ❌ "Decentralized evidence" | Misleading | ✅ "Independently verifiable" |
| ❌ "Immutable blockchain" | Conflates with crypto | ✅ "Append-only chain" |
| ❌ "Web3 AI governance" | Hype terminology | ✅ "Cryptographic AI governance" |

---

## Context-Specific Prohibitions

### Website

Never display:
- Compliance badges we haven't earned
- "Certified" or "Approved" labels
- Guarantees of regulatory acceptance
- Claims about decision quality

### UI

Never show:
- "AI Approved ✓"
- "Compliance Status: Compliant"
- "This decision is correct"
- "Fraud prevented"

### Sales Materials

Never say:
- "This makes you compliant"
- "Regulators will accept this"
- "You won't face enforcement"
- "This proves your AI is fair"

### Press/Media

Never claim:
- "We regulate AI"
- "We prevent AI harms"
- "We make AI safe"
- "We replace auditors"

---

## Violation Consequences

| Severity | Example | Response |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Critical | "Guarantees compliance" on website | Immediate removal, incident report |
| High | "Certified" in sales deck | Same-day correction, review |
| Medium | Ambiguous phrasing in docs | Scheduled correction |
| Low | Informal chat hyperbole | Coaching |

---

## Correction Procedure

When a prohibited claim is found:

1. **Identify** - Document the claim and location
2. **Remove** - Immediately if critical/high
3. **Replace** - Use approved alternative
4. **Review** - Check for similar issues
5. **Document** - Log the incident
