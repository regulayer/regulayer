# Sales Misuse Constraints

**Rules of Engagement for Selling Regulayer**

## The Golden Rule
Do not sell what the math cannot deliver.

## Forbidden Phrases
- Do not say: "Guarantee Compliance"
- Do not say: "Regulayer Certification"
- Do not say: "Bias Prevention"
- Do not say: "Legal Shield"

## Required Disclaimers
When presenting to Legal/Compliance buyers, you MUST state:
"Regulayer provides the technical layer of integrity. Your internal governance provides the compliance."
