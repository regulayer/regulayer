# Misleading Claims

**Common Falsehoods & Corrections**

| False Claim | Correction |
| :--- | :--- |
| "Regulayer ensures AI Safety." | "Regulayer ensures AI Auditability." |
| "This decision is Verified Correct." | "This decision Record is Verified Authentic." |
| "We cannot lie because of Regulayer." | "If we lie, the tampering will be detectable." |
| "Regulayer stores our data." | "Regulayer stores hashes of your data." |
| "Regulayer is an approved regulator." | "Regulayer is a technology provider." |
| "We had proof at the time." | "Verification proves authenticity of a record, not that it existed at a claimed time unless supported by Time Anchors." |

## Zero Tolerance
Partners found systematically using the "False Claim" column in marketing materials will face contract termination under the **Misuse Prevention Policy**.
