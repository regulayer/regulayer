### Technical Background

10. **What is Regulayer?**
    Regulayer is a service that creates cryptographic records of
    AI system decisions. Each record is:
    - Hashed using SHA-256
    - Signed using Ed25519 digital signatures
    - Linked in an append-only chain

11. **What does "cryptographically verifiable" mean?**
    It means that mathematical techniques can prove:
    - Content has not been modified
    - The record was created by a specific key holder
    - Records are in a specific order
